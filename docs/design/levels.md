# Campaign and Levels

The campaign is linear at the map level. Choices produce scene variants and tactical assistance so
the story can acknowledge the player without requiring parallel production. Each tactical mission
introduces at most one enemy role and one map concept before recombining known pieces.

## Act I — Receiving

### 0. Before the Turn

- **Place:** Turnward depot and adjacent civic shell
- **Objective:** inspect three thresholds and move residents before a routine test shift
- **Teaches:** movement, interaction, cover, visible shifts, the crew's ordinary work
- **Story:** Nacre is introduced as a practiced home before it becomes a crisis
- **Combat:** none; a failed brace creates a rescue, not an enemy

### 1. Receiving Light

- **Place:** contact dock and market threshold
- **Objective:** escort Safekeeping surveyors to a damaged anchor, then extract when custody begins
- **Teaches:** strikes, Guard, incapacitation, retrieval officers
- **Story:** outside contact reveals the hidden forecast; Renn declares emergency custody
- **Choice:** preserve survey equipment or use it to open the fastest escape

### 2. Emergency Custody

- **Place:** occupied Receiving concourse
- **Objective:** open a route for Crew Seven and recover the public transit keys
- **Teaches:** declared intents, marks, brace carriers, alternate exits
- **Story:** the crew chooses organized resistance over compliance or flight
- **Choice:** release detained Departure Circle members at tactical cost

## Act II — Custody

### 3. The Unreceived

- **Place:** residential shell during partial transition
- **Objective:** reach households omitted from the official evacuation register
- **Teaches:** restrained units, drones, Assist, first live adjacency shift
- **Story:** Safekeeping triage has classified difficult-to-reach residents as already lost
- **Choice:** protect an enemy medic treating local and Safekeeping casualties

### 4. Willing Passage

- **Place:** orchard reservoir route to a civilian transport
- **Objective:** escort Ari Tann and voluntary evacuees through conflict between occupiers and locals
- **Teaches:** moving escort group, local auxiliaries, non-hostile third-party movement
- **Story:** leaving is established as a legitimate choice; custody threatens it too
- **Choice:** publish the passenger list, seal it, or entrust it to Ari; this changes later testimony

### 5. The Quiet Map

- **Place:** civic archive folded behind an obsolete threshold
- **Objective:** recover transition records and transmit the concealed forecast
- **Teaches:** surveyor lanes, evidence interactions, optional stealth route without a stealth system
- **Story:** the crew proves both local institutional failure and the outsiders' incomplete model
- **Choice:** release the full record immediately or first redact residents' private medical routes

### 6. A Safe Shape

- **Place:** stabilizer works across two connected map chambers
- **Objective:** disable three lock relays before the geometry freezes
- **Teaches:** lock engineers, linked devices, linebreaker rig
- **Story:** Nera defects; Vale recognizes that stabilization is causing damage
- **Choice:** preserve Vale's equipment and team or destroy the network more quickly

## Act III — The Turn

### 7. Terms of Survival

- **Place:** Assembly chamber and public relay
- **Objective:** keep an open assembly broadcasting until three testimony rounds finish
- **Teaches:** field coordinators, defensive movement, surrender cascades
- **Story:** locals depose the emergency committee; Safekeeping personnel hear contrary evidence
- **Choice:** who speaks last—Mora, Ari, Nera, or a recovered resident—changes who joins the finale

### 8. Homeomorph

- **Place:** central anchor during the full Long Turn
- **Objective:** repair three anchors, disable Renn's lock, and preserve a voluntary passage lane
- **Teaches:** mastery only; two previewed shifts recombine all established tools
- **Story:** Nacre survives by changing; the expedition splits over Renn's final order
- **Variation:** previous choices determine helpers, shortcuts, surrender options, and repair methods

## Epilogue — Aftershape

- **Place:** the same depot from the prologue, now connected to an unfamiliar public garden
- **Objective:** walk the repaired district, speak to five people, and submit Crew Seven's public report
- **Play:** no combat; the player chooses the report's final recommendation after seeing consequences
- **Story:** departures, accountability, reforms, and unresolved relationships receive specific endings

## Reuse plan

The ten sequences use six base maps and four environment kits. The depot returns in the epilogue;
Receiving supports missions 1 and 2; residential/civic tiles support 3 and 7; archive/works tiles
support 5 and 6; the central anchor reuses Turn Interior assets established throughout. Changed
routes, lighting, population, and objectives create narrative transformation without unique art for
every scene.
