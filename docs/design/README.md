# Homeomorph Design Index

## The game in one sentence

A deterministic, story-led tactical RPG about five habitat workers resisting compulsory rescue
during the periodic spatial transformation that makes their home possible.

## Player promise

Homeomorph gives the player a strange place to understand through familiar, highly legible
tactics. Every mission reveals how Nacre works, why the Safekeeping believes occupation is mercy,
and what each protagonist considers worth preserving. Victory is not proving that one society was
always right; it is creating enough room for informed choices before irreversible action.

## Fixed shape

The campaign contains a prologue, eight further tactical missions, and a playable epilogue. The
player controls a fixed roster of five and deploys four. The maps are authored, the outcomes are
deterministic, and the story rejoins after local choices. A first complete run should take six to
eight hours.

The names Nacre, the Safekeeping, and all named characters are Homeomorph proposals. They are not
automatically broader Glass Frontier canon. See [the canon boundary](../canon-boundary.md).
