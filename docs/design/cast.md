# Cast

The roster contains exactly five playable characters. Four deploy per tactical mission. A required
mission never assumes a specific optional deployment; protagonist-specific interactions provide
advantages or alternate routes rather than mandatory completion keys.

## Mara Ell — Turn captain

Mara coordinates Crew Seven and knows how to hold a plan together while the space around it
changes. She signed an emergency routing order based on the Assembly's concealed forecast and did
not ask why several neighborhoods were quietly deprioritized.

- **Combat identity:** anchor and coordinator; protects positions and transfers actions.
- **Need:** distinguish responsibility from obedience.
- **Arc:** from containing uncertainty to making risk and authority public.
- **Voice:** precise, dry, attentive to the person who has not spoken.

## Iven Ro — route-reader

Iven reads adjacency cues faster than anyone on the crew. He loves Nacre without believing that
staying proves devotion. His older sibling left through an illicit passage years ago and has never
been permitted to send an official message home.

- **Combat identity:** scout and repositioner; reveals routes and exploits marked targets.
- **Need:** stop treating departure and abandonment as the same wound.
- **Arc:** from private restlessness to building legitimate contact beyond Nacre.
- **Voice:** irreverent, observant, first to name an absurdity.

## Sela Om — medic and civic liaison

Sela treats Turn injuries and mediates the arguments that follow them. She has worked with the
Departure Circle and sees the arrival of safe ships as a genuine opportunity until custody makes
choice meaningless.

- **Combat identity:** recovery and de-escalation; clears conditions and converts surrender states.
- **Need:** act decisively without becoming another person deciding for others.
- **Arc:** from neutral facilitator to advocate for enforceable consent.
- **Voice:** patient but not gentle by default; asks concrete questions.

## Tarn Vey — structure technician

Tarn maintains old anchors with parts the Assembly pretends are temporary. He has warned about the
degradation for years and distrusts both outside certainty and local ceremony.

- **Combat identity:** builder and breacher; creates cover, opens paths, disables machines.
- **Need:** turn justified distrust into structures other people can share.
- **Arc:** from solitary repair to transparent civic stewardship.
- **Voice:** blunt, materially specific, unexpectedly delighted by good workmanship.

## Nera Quill — retrieval officer

Nera enters as a Safekeeping officer and becomes playable after refusing an order to destroy local
records. She is not a naive newcomer: she has participated in coercive evacuations that saved lives
and one that she now suspects did not need to happen.

- **Combat identity:** interdictor and intelligence specialist; redirects intents and disables command.
- **Need:** replace institutional innocence with accountable loyalty.
- **Arc:** from proving the doctrine works to accepting the people it failed as authorities.
- **Voice:** formal, candid, uncomfortable with reassurance she cannot evidence.

## Principal non-player characters

### Commander Ossa Renn

The expedition commander and central antagonist. Renn survived a failed evacuation as a child and
has since learned that hesitation can be disguised as respect. She listens seriously, changes
tactics when evidence changes, and cannot accept that residents may choose a risk she can prevent.

### Convenor Jalen Mora

Nacre's public coordinator. Mora concealed the anchor forecast to avoid panic and uncontrolled
departure. He remains useful during the crisis but must not be vindicated merely because the
invaders are worse.

### Ari Tann

A Departure Circle organizer who secures places on the first transports. Ari opposes compulsory
removal and also refuses pressure to perform loyalty by staying. Protecting Ari's convoy makes the
game's commitment to agency tangible.

### Kero Vale

The Safekeeping's senior stabilization engineer. Vale initially treats Nacre expertise as anecdote,
then recognizes that the expedition's own lock is damaging the hab. Preserving Vale's team creates
an additional repair option in the finale.

### Eni Ro

Iven's older sibling, now a junior navigator on a civilian ship shadowing the expedition. Their
delayed messages show that the world outside Nacre is neither salvation nor betrayal.
