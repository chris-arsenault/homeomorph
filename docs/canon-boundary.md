# Canon Boundary

Homeomorph is an exploration inside the Glass Frontier, not a replacement summary of it. This file
exists so game production can invent freely without accidentally narrowing the wider setting.

## Inherited setting frame

The following concepts are treated as established for this project:

- the Glass Frontier contains connected yet radically different habitats;
- millions of habitats may remain undiscovered;
- physical reality can behave differently inside them;
- the setting exists to support stories across varied places and degrees of realism;
- new games and players are expected to add to a canon that is not complete;
- hope-oriented tone is a preference and guide, not a single mandatory cosmic thesis;
- the Bloom and Shear are parts of the setting, not required centers of every story.

Exact spellings, definitions, chronology, and cross-setting implications remain owned by the
canonical lore repository. If this summary conflicts with accepted canon, accepted canon wins.

## Homeomorph proposals

Everything specific below originates in this game and remains proposal canon until deliberately
accepted upstream:

- Nacre and the Long Turn;
- Turnward Crew Seven and all named characters;
- the Safekeeping, its history, doctrine, technology, and expedition;
- the Departure Circle and Nacre Assembly;
- the anchor failure, emergency custody, Quiet Map, stabilization plan, and outcome;
- any implied travel time, contact practice, or relationship to other habitats.

## Rules for writers and agents

- Build outward from one hab's lived specificity; do not make its conflict secretly define the universe.
- Do not explain the Long Turn through the Bloom or Shear merely because those concepts already exist.
- Introduce outside facts only when this plot needs them, and label unresolved facts as proposals.
- Use wider canon for constraints and resonance, not cameo accumulation.
- Keep inhabitants internally diverse. No faction speaks for everyone who shares a home or history.
- Do not upstream names or facts automatically. Prepare a focused canon proposal for human review.

## Upstream review packet

Before canon integration, produce a short proposal containing: the exact new facts required by the
released story; conflicts found during a fresh canon review; terms that can remain game-local; and
hooks deliberately left open. Canon balancing is a separate decision from finishing the game.
