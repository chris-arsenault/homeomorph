### Added

- Playable gray-box campaign spanning all ten sequences, with deployment, thirty skill choices,
  objective progression, story decisions, finale assistance, and personal epilogue accounts.
- Deterministic commands, inspectable enemy order and paths, conditions, surrender, safe habitat
  shifts, reinforcements, difficulty customization, and activation rewind.
- Keyboard and pointer board controls, exact action confirmation, scalable text, and local save
  import/export with corruption and storage-failure handling.
- Campaign completion, roster, mechanics, save-boundary, and browser-control regression tests.

- Design foundation for story, cast, factions, missions, combat, skills, progression, difficulty,
  art direction, and production scope.
- Typed browser reference for campaign, character, enemy, and skill definitions.
- Deterministic combat preview and authored-content validation with unit tests.
- Ahara-compatible CI, Terraform, deployment, documentation, and repository structure.
